# Velocity-informed hold envelope coverage on development packets

Frozen before scoring the trajectories. This CPU analysis uses the six existing zero-correction captures from PR #40 at commit `b814702456011974e59d9afe2568096208794b1e`. Each channel contributes the same eight uncorrected walks at each fault gain. The experiment performs no new training, tuning, action selection or paid simulation.

* Test every constant acceleration limit in 25, 50, 100, 200, 400, 800 and 1600 rad/s². Do not optimize or select a limit. Compare the current 10 rad/s velocity-independent hold envelope at identical samples.
* At decision k use only the latest accepted RLS packet. Its source s is a post-transition sample. Its age is (k−1−s) dt. Use its encoder reading q_s and reported velocity v_s. Use the declared position error b_q for that sensor condition and b_v=0. Both archived channels pass simulator velocity through without error. Physical velocity and timing uncertainty require independent characterization for any other sensor.
* At elapsed time u, bound position by q_s + v_s u ± (b_q + A u²/2). The age-to-age-plus-hold enclosing interval uses the smaller lower and larger upper endpoint at those two elapsed times. The lower function is concave in u and the upper is convex, so both extrema occur at an endpoint. For the speed-only comparison use q_s ± (b_q + 10 u) and the corresponding enclosing interval.
* Primary observations are post-onset, live, nonnegative-age hip and knee joint decisions from onset through +1.00 s inclusive. Also report the entire live post-onset horizon. Compare the two authentic position observations (decision and end-of-hold) with the interval at each corresponding instant and with the enclosing hold interval. Count both-observed coverage, failures and excess, conditional on exposure; missing source data and true simulation termination are separately reported. A covered pair of control-tick positions can miss an interior excursion.
* Report group, gain, channel, packet-age and method summaries including joint-step exposure, mean hold-interval width and fraction with error sign determined relative to the current reference. For descriptive acceleration evidence, compute |v[k]−v[k−1]|/dt from consecutive observed exact source packet velocities, grouped by gain and joint. This reports a control-tick average, with contact transients and within-tick peaks unresolved. No held-out split or controller decision uses these measurements.
