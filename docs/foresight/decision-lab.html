Calculation, assumptions and counts

The time-to-contact rule (TTC) uses a 0.3 s threshold. The lead-conditioned belief rule uses preparation cost 0.05 internally. The reactive walker never activates BRACE. Both active rules switch between frozen skills. The price slider scores these fixed policies after the experiment.

Cost per encounter = probability of a fall + penalty × probability of bracing on a scheduled near-miss. A preparation is an episode with any recorded bracing. A scheduled near-miss may still make physical contact. Duration and walking progress have no additional cost in this calculation.

Changing the mix reweights the hit and near-miss strata. It assumes unchanged within-stratum behavior, including the mix of object masses, paths and speeds. The experiment used simulated detections at 10 Hz. The transfer check uses different trained skills and the development scenario schedule.
