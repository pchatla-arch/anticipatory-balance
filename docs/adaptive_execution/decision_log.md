## Recording the next decision

Append an ID, date, named decision-maker, exact decision (GO/MODIFY/STOP or
merge/claim approval), scope, immutable evidence/memo references and budget if
applicable. Retain earlier decisions. If a decision is superseded, append the
new decision and identify the old one; do not rewrite the original as a success.

A message may be recorded as evidence when Prasuna explicitly gives the
decision. Git author metadata alone does not prove that Prasuna approved a
technical claim. The document checker verifies the presence of a declared
decision record, not a person's identity or the scientific validity of it.
