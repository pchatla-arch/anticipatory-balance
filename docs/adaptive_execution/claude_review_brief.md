# Independent review request

Please review the detailed technical design paper **Fleet Robust Execution**, version 0.2, in this directory: [paper.md](paper.md) or [paper.pdf](paper.pdf). It incorporates the review of the four-page November 15, 2026 Fleet-Robust specification. The corrected detailed paper is authoritative for equations, rates, interfaces and evidence status; the original specification supplies the fixed component boundary.

The selected architecture is fixed: a frozen G1 PPO position-target policy; a bring-up calibration option; online actuator estimation; bounded target correction; confidence and fault supervision; and a C++ implementation of the estimator/corrector. A learned estimator or residual is a controlled comparison or a targeted extension within this boundary. Review correctness and implementation readiness. Propose an architectural change only if a concrete contradiction makes the stated capability impossible; explain the smallest correction that preserves the boundary.

At the document cutoff, GPU recovery, calibration, online identification, confidence logic, learned adapters, the C++ runtime and the task-cycle demonstration remain proposed or unverified. Attribute any newer evidence to its actual commit, checkpoint and run manifest.

Please audit the following:

1. **Physics and correction.** Match MuJoCo's proportional actuator with separate joint damping. Verify consistent control-gain and position-bias scaling, the inverse formula, physical target clipping and the distinction between correction slew and total-target slew. Check assumptions of any torque-error proposition and its sample-and-hold limitations.
2. **Telemetry and identifiability.** Verify actual sent command, sampled joint state and effort semantics and timestamps. Exact simulated effort must be diagnostic, not evidence of difficult identification. Review noisy/delayed and no-effort conditions, weak excitation, contact/load confounds and contact-aware update eligibility.
3. **Information boundaries.** Prevent gain, onset, future state, simulator contact truth and privileged critic information from reaching deployed inference. Calibration and any additional sensors need explicit acquisition assumptions and costs.
4. **Confidence and supervision.** Distinguish an RLS covariance proxy from calibrated uncertainty. Verify stale-data handling, model rejection, actuator authority and requested versus effective constraints. Returning to a faulty base policy must not be described as a proven safe fallback.
5. **Timing and C++.** Verify 50 Hz policy timing, 500 Hz simulation timing and the first C++ parity target. If 1 kHz remains a milestone, specify what is updated at 1 kHz and how plant dynamics are represented. Separate empirical latency from hard real-time or hardware-in-the-loop claims.
6. **Deep RL comparisons.** Compare classical estimation, learned history estimation, residual PPO and actuator-randomized PPO with declared sensing, correction authority, training budgets and held-out distributions. Retain the same actor for adapter comparisons; a retrained PPO baseline is a different comparison family.
7. **Evaluation and statistics.** Pair exogenous scenarios while allowing controller-dependent state trajectories. Retain failures and fixed horizons. Review healthy noninferiority margins, oracle-gap denominator validity, policy-seed provenance, numerical failures and reproducible checkpoint recovery.
8. **Claim traceability.** Keep “fleet” as motivation until heterogeneous-instance generalization is measured. Absence of gain randomization does not prove intolerance. Training cost and scalability must be compared. Mark every module as implemented, checked, measured or proposed; distinguish prior art from a contribution to be established.

Return: an overall readiness assessment; findings with severity, exact section/code reference, mechanism and minimal correction; corrected mathematical statements with assumptions; the smallest experiments needed to resolve each uncertainty; and the strongest supportable contribution statement. Mark **blocking** only for invalid physics, information leakage, a false proof or an experiment incapable of testing its claim; **major** for consequential comparison/evidence gaps; **minor** for clarity and implementation detail. Separate verified defects from hypotheses. Supply proposed edits or patches for reconciliation against the shared repository; review alone should not launch paid jobs, install packages, or alter published history.
