## Answers to the eight questions

1. **Model consistency.** The unit-gear proportional servo with separate damping is exactly MuJoCo's `position` actuator with the gain/bias scaling used by the fault wrapper; `qfrc_actuator` at the joint DoF after limits is the right effort reference (AE-001/002, verified in `telemetry.py`). The force-error proposition is correct as stated (algebra re-derived; corner extremality holds because the bracket is affine in g and in e separately). Its scope is instantaneous force, not motion; see R6.
2. **Mathematical consistency.** Clipping preimage, requested vs effective, headroom and slew are consistent in `projection.py` (verified in PR #34 review). The fallback is consistent on paper but lives outside the primitives (R5).
3. **Authority.** Unknown; the single most important unmeasured quantity (R1). The smallest experiment is an offline interval replay on recorded packets; it needs no GPU.
4. **Abrupt jumps.** Treated honestly in the text. The return-to-healthy case is now measured for the baselines (R3) and is the sharpest test of the candidate's reacquisition rule, which must be specified before G2.
5. **Information assumptions.** Exact-effort diagnostics, bounded-error assumptions and empirical coverage are now separable in the bench (exact vs declared channel; coverage counts pending). Genuine sensor ambiguity (scale) is measured and unfixable by any effort-only estimator (R2).
6. **Distinction.** From tuned gating: the candidate's abstention is derived from declared bounds and age, not tuned; the test is whether that derivation beats tuning at matched budget (G2). From a smaller fixed cap: the proposal already names it as a baseline; it should be run (it is one line in the bench: a method with max_correction_rad reduced). From SAG/TeAR/set-membership control: the interface (position targets, finite holds, declared telemetry quality) and the harmful-intervention side of the tradeoff are the distinction; the paper must say the modules are established.
7. **Learning comparisons.** Robust PPO is fair if it gets the same action envelope, permitted history and a recorded budget. The GRU comparison is likely to show "projected away" (R7). Residual PPO should wait for a measured limitation; none is measured yet.
8. **Recovery vs inaction.** The bench separates them: `postfault_loss` on fault families vs `harmful_offset_rad` on healthy families, paired, with `none` and `oracle` as anchors; a method that never acts scores like `none` on the first and like `oracle` on the second, so the frontier plot (loss vs harmful offset) exposes it. Held-out splits exist (dev/val/test seeds); held-out policy seeds need WP 3.3.

## Readiness, risk, and the next evidence gate

- **Implementation readiness.** Primitives (WP 1.2) and bench (WP 1.1/1.4/2.1/2.3/4.1) exist and are CPU-tested on the pod's versions. WP 2.2 (candidate in the loop, reacquisition and fallback) is the remaining integration step; the hook is defined.
- **Research risk.** R1 (no authority for mild faults) and R2 (scale confound) are the two ways the candidate ends up equal to "do nothing" or equal to the heuristic. Both are measurable on CPU this week.
- **Learning contribution.** Robust PPO is the one learning comparison with a clear interpretation; schedule it; keep the GRU conditional on R7's count.
- **Smallest next evidence gate (G1, by Nov 25):** offline authority replay on the dev-bench packets for both telemetry channels: interval width, time to exclude g = 1, permitted |x| vs the oracle's |x|, violation and reset counts. Go if permitted correction reaches ≥ 50% of the oracle's within 1 s of onset for g ≤ 0.70 under declared telemetry; modify (declare a relaxed variant) if only 0.55 clears; stop the research claim if nothing does.

## Measurements that would change the supported claim or envelope

- Permitted correction under declared bounds (R1) decides whether the claim covers 0.85, 0.70 or only 0.55.
- Withdrawal steps after return (R3) decides whether "fewer harmful interventions" includes the recovery case or only the healthy-noise case.
- The scale family (R2) stays a stated limitation regardless of result.
- If the tuned heuristic matches the candidate on the registered test, the deliverable is the component and the benchmark (the proposal's own fallback), and the paper's title drops "identification-aware".
